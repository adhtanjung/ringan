## Design Context

### Users
System administrators and data maintainers, specifically including elders/older individuals. The primary context is straightforward data management and system upkeep.

### Brand Personality
Clear, Accessible, Forgiving. The interface should evoke a sense of calm and confidence, ensuring that users do not feel overwhelmed by complex data operations.

### Aesthetic Direction
The design must prioritize extreme ease of use. It should feature high legibility, large interactive targets, and a straightforward, uncluttered layout. It should explicitly avoid dense, highly technical, or visually noisy interfaces.

### Design Principles
1. **Clarity over Density**: Avoid packing too much information into a single view. Use generous spacing and clear typography (avoiding overly small text) to aid aging eyes.
2. **Obvious Interactions**: Interactive elements must be easily identifiable with high contrast and large, easily clickable areas (minimum 44x44px touch targets).
3. **Progressive Disclosure**: Keep the primary interface incredibly simple, revealing complex data management options only when explicitly needed.
4. **Forgiving Architecture**: Ensure errors are easy to recognize and recover from, utilizing clear, jargon-free explanations.
